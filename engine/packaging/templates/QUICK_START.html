__DISPLAY_NAME__

This folder contains everything the report needs. There is nothing to install.

  1. Double-click START.bat.
  2. Your browser opens the report automatically. Leave the small window that appears open while you work.
  3. Drag your Excel files onto the page, or press the box to choose them.
  4. Press Process and watch the progress.
  5. Read the result: PASS means every total matched, WARNING means some rows need attention, and BLOCK means nothing was published and your previous result was kept.
  6. Use the dashboard, download a CSV, or press Print.

To close the report, close the browser tab and the small window.

If something goes wrong: the page tells you what happened, whether your previous results are still safe, and the one thing to do next. It also shows a support code such as E-IN-001. Send that code to your support contact.

Want it to update itself? Double-click SCHEDULE.bat once and choose how often - every hour, once a day, or off. You do not need to keep this folder open for it to work. If an automatic update ever needs your attention, a file named NEEDS_ATTENTION.txt appears in this folder to tell you what happened and what to do.

Everything runs on this computer. No internet connection is used and no file leaves the machine.
